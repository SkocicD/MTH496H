\begin{frame}{Why Does the Crust Work?}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item The Voronoi vertices approximate the medial axis
		\item A circle connecting two adjacent points in the crust must not contain a Voronoi vertex by the empty circle property
		\item This means that the crust can't "cut across the middle" of the shape
	\end{itemize}
